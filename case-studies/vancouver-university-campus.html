All case studies

Project
Vancouver university campus building
Role
Mitacs Intern & Graduate Research Assistant, CREATE Lab, Simon Fraser University, since January 2025. Mitacs-funded industry partnership with a Vancouver post-secondary institution.
Building type
University campus building in Vancouver, with complex geometry, sloped architectural elements, and varying floor-to-floor heights
Tool
IES VE, with imported AutoCAD drawings.
Modelled
Classrooms, offices and common areas each have their own occupancy, lighting and equipment profiles.
Compared
Working from imported AutoCAD drawings, I compared 25 HVAC configurations on heating load, cooling load and GHGI, and showed how envelope and construction choices change energy use and emissions. My favourite part is showing how small design changes lead to big results.
Future climate
For the same building, each HVAC system was also simulated with 2040, 2050 and 2060 weather files. Future-climate resilience analysis.
