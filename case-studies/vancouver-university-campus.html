Project
Vancouver university campus building
Role
Mitacs Intern & Graduate Research Assistant, CREATE Lab, Simon Fraser University, since January 2025. Mitacs-funded industry partnership with a Vancouver post-secondary institution.
Building type
University campus building in Vancouver, with complex geometry, sloped architectural elements, and varying floor-to-floor heights
Tool
IES VE [software version: confirm]
Goal
Find scenarios that cut campus energy use and support net-zero goals.
Modelled
Geometry, thermal zones, envelope assemblies, glazing, shading, internal gains, and HVAC. Classrooms, offices, and common areas each have occupancy, lighting, computer and equipment loads, with annual variation in student occupancy and campus use.
Scenarios
25 HVAC configurations, plus envelope and shading alternatives: glazing properties, tinting, and internal and external shading. Each option was screened on TEDI, CEDI, TEUI, and GHGI.
Results

Design scenarios were identified that reduce campus energy use in support of net-zero goals. The size of that reduction is not on this page yet.
