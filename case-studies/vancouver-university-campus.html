Project
Vancouver university campus building
Role
Mitacs Intern & Graduate Research Assistant, CREATE Lab, Simon Fraser University, since January 2025. Mitacs-funded industry partnership with a Vancouver post-secondary institution.
Building type
A roughly 12,600 m², highly glazed university campus building in Vancouver, with complex geometry, sloped architectural elements and varying floor-to-floor heights. Classrooms, offices and common areas each have their own occupancy, lighting and equipment profiles.
Tools
IES VE and ApacheHVAC, working from imported AutoCAD drawings.
HVAC
Detailed ApacheHVAC networks for 25 system configurations, from boiler-chiller VAV and fan coils to air-source heat pumps with DOAS and heat recovery and VRF. The configurations were ranked with an equal-weight score across TEDI, CEDI, TEUI and GHGI.
Envelope
On two HVAC bases, VRF and heat pump fan coils, 15 envelope retrofit cases, including insulation, high-performance glazing, lower SHGC, solar-controlled external louvres and blinds.
Standards
NECB 2020, the NECB 2025 performance path, and the BC Energy Step Code.
Future climate
The same HVAC systems were also simulated with PCIC morphed CMIP6 (SSP1-2.6) weather files for the 2040s, 2050s and 2060s. Future-climate resilience analysis.

My favourite part is showing how small design changes lead to big results.
