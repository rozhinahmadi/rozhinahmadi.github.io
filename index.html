I'm a building performance analyst and MASc candidate in Sustainable Energy Engineering at Simon Fraser University, based in Metro Vancouver. I build whole-building energy models in IES VE, from geometry, thermal zones and envelope assemblies to glazing, shading, internal loads and HVAC systems, and I turn the results into clear metrics such as TEDI, TEUI and GHGI, checked against NECB 2020 and BC Energy Step Code requirements, that help design teams make better decisions.

I also test designs against future climate, using 2040–2060 weather files, and support embodied-carbon work through life cycle assessment. My goal is simple: help buildings in BC use less energy and emit less carbon, from early design through to net-zero.

Campus energy model

Vancouver university campus building

Mitacs Intern & Graduate Research Assistant, CREATE Lab, Simon Fraser University · since January 2025

Through a Mitacs-funded industry partnership with a Vancouver post-secondary institution, I modelled a roughly 12,600 m², highly glazed university campus building in IES VE, working from imported AutoCAD drawings. I built detailed ApacheHVAC networks for 25 system configurations, from boiler-chiller VAV and fan coils to air-source heat pumps with DOAS and heat recovery and VRF, then ranked them with an equal-weight score across TEDI, CEDI, TEUI and GHGI. On two HVAC bases (VRF and heat pump fan coils) I tested 15 envelope retrofit cases, including insulation, high-performance glazing, lower SHGC, solar-controlled external louvres and blinds. The study follows NECB 2020, the NECB 2025 performance path and the BC Energy Step Code.

Study of Toxic Air Contaminants Exposure and Evacuation Zone for an Oil Tank Pool Fire in a Residential Neighborhood

Canadian Society for Mechanical Engineering International Congress 2026 · AERMOD View

Using AERMOD View (Lakes Environmental), I modelled CO and PM2.5 dispersion from an oil tank pool fire across Pasquill–Gifford stability classes, compared exposure with BC Ambient Air Quality Objectives and assessed the evacuation zone needed to protect residents.
